\subsection{Mean Free Path}
\begin{theory}
The mean free path $\lambda$ of a photon in a medium is given by:
\begin{equation}
    \lambda = \frac{1}{n\sigma},
\end{equation}
where $n$ is the number density of scatterers and $\sigma$ is the scattering cross-section.
\end{theory}
